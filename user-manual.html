Glan White Sand Beach Resort

Web-based Payroll Management System

User Manual — Start-to-End Navigation Flow

STEP 1 — Start the System

Open PowerShell (or any terminal) in the project folder, run the dev server, then open your browser.

  1. Run: npm run dev
  2. Open: http://localhost:3000 (or :3002 depending on your .env)
  3. You will be redirected automatically to the Sign-In page at /auth/signin.

STEP 2 — Sign In

Use one of the demo accounts. The middleware reads your role and routes you to the correct dashboard.

STEP 3 — ADMIN Flow (HR / Full Setup)

After login as Admin, the sidebar shows 12 menus. Follow this order: set up data first, then run daily operations, then process payroll, then view analytics.

A. Initial Setup (do these once, in order)
  1. Dashboard (/) — Verify totals, attendance trends, departments overview.
  2. Departments (/departments) — Create departments (Front Office, Housekeeping, F&B, etc.).
  3. Position Salaries (/position-salaries) — Define each position's base salary. The system is position-based, not SG-based.
  4. Schedules (/schedules) — Create work shifts (Regular Day, Night, Weekend, Flexible).
  5. Holidays (/holidays) — Add regular and special holidays.
  6. CA Settings (/cash-advance-settings) — Set cash-advance limits and rules.
  7. Benefits (/benefits) — Configure SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax.
  8. Employees (/employees) — Add each employee, assign position/department/schedule, upload profile picture, and enroll their face for face-recognition attendance.
B. Daily Operations
  1. Attendance (/attendance) — Kiosk + admin screen. Employees scan their face (or QR fallback) to Time In / Time Out. Admin can review, correct, or manually add logs. Late, undertime, and overtime are auto-computed.
  2. Requests (/department-head-requests) — Review, approve, or reject Overtime, Cash Advance, and Leave that bubble up from Department Heads.
C. Payroll Processing (semi-monthly)
  1. Payroll (/payroll): create a Payroll Period (start → end date), click Generate. The system pulls attendance, OT, CA, deductions, and holidays, then computes net pay. Review each employee, export the payslip to PDF, and mark the period as paid / archived.
D. Reporting
  1. Analytics (/analytics) — Attendance trends, payroll cost charts, department insights.

STEP 4 — DEPARTMENT HEAD Flow

After login as Department Head, the sidebar is split into three sections: Payroll, Request (approve team), and My Requests (their own).

Section 1 — Payroll
  1. Dashboard (/department-head-dashboard) — Overview of their department.
  2. Department Attendance (/department-head-dashboard/department-attendance) — View their team's DTR.
  3. Department Payroll (/department-head-dashboard/department-payroll) — View team's payroll summary.
  4. My DTR (/department-head-dashboard/my-dtr) — Their own time records.
  5. My Payslip (/department-head-dashboard/my-payslip) — Download their own payslip.
Section 2 — Approve Team Requests
  1. Overtime Request (/department-head-dashboard/requests/overtime) — Approve or reject OT from their staff.
  2. Leave Request (/department-head-dashboard/requests/leave) — Approve or reject leave. Cash advances typically escalate directly to Admin.
Section 3 — Their Own Requests
  1. My Overtime (/department-head-dashboard/my-requests/overtime) — Submit their own OT.
  2. My Cash Advance (/department-head-dashboard/my-requests/cash-advance) — Submit CA.
  3. My Leave (/department-head-dashboard/my-requests/leave) — Submit leave with attachment.

Department Heads can also access /schedules to plan shifts for upcoming months (allowed by middleware).

STEP 5 — EMPLOYEE Flow

After login as Employee, the sidebar shows 6 items. Employees do not time in/out from their own dashboard — they walk up to the shared kiosk device.

  1. Dashboard (/employee-dashboard) — Personal stats and upcoming schedule.
  2. My Attendance (/employee-dashboard/my-attendance) — DTR-style view of past time-in/out, late, OT, calendar view.
  3. My Payslip (/employee-dashboard/my-payslip) — Semi-monthly payslip. Base = Monthly Salary ÷ 2, auto-adjusted for late/absent/OT. Download as PDF.
  4. Overtime (/employee-dashboard/overtime) — Apply for OT, routed to Department Head.
  5. Cash Advance (/employee-dashboard/cash-advance) — Apply for CA, routed to Admin.
  6. Leave (/employee-dashboard/leave) — Apply for leave with letter/proof attachment.
Punching Attendance (kiosk)
  • Employees go to a shared kiosk device (tablet/PC at the resort entrance) which opens /attendance.
  • Kiosk endpoints (/api/attendance/qr-scan and /api/attendance/enrolled-faces) are public — no login required.
  • The face-attendance component matches their face against the enrolled photos and logs Time In or Time Out automatically.

STEP 6 — End-of-Day / End-of-Period

  1. Admin reviews /attendance for any missing punches and corrects them.
  2. Department Heads clear pending requests in their queue.
  3. Admin generates payroll in /payroll at the end of each semi-monthly cycle.
  4. Employees download their payslip from /employee-dashboard/my-payslip.
  5. Everyone uses the avatar menu (top-right) → Sign out to end the session.
